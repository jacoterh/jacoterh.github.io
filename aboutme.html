About me

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in particle physics and my research is centred around three main pillars: model independent searches for new physics through the Standard Model Effective Field Theory (SMEFT), the determination of the inner structure of the proton using advanced machine learning techniques, and the assessment of the physics potential of future colliders, all connected through cutting-edge theoretical modelling techniques.

I am a member of the NNPDF collaboration where we study the structure and contents of the proton using Machine Learning techniques. I am also one of the core developers of SMEFiT, a python package developed to perform global analyses of LHC data to search for physics beyond the Standard Model in a model-independent way. I obtained my PhD at the Vrije Universiteit in Amsterdam and Nikhef supervised by Juan Rojo.

I am broadly interested in the phenomenology of the Standard Model, the Standard Model Effective Field Theory (SMEFT), global SMEFT fits, ultraviolet (UV) matching and the physics potential of future colliders. I am also involved in an interdisciplinary project with the TU Delft where we use ML techniques to study electronic properties of nano-layered materials. Beyond research I am passionate about art and some of my works you might already have found when landing on this website.
